# Landing experience review

Review the running page, not just its source. Check desktop and narrow/mobile viewports, initial load, middle sections, anchor navigation, the primary CTA, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior. Verify that oversized typography does not clip important words and overlapping panels do not hide content.

Trace product numbers, benefits, testimonials, and legal claims to supplied or cited sources. Flag assumptions explicitly. Check font/image licenses and avoid distributing unlicensed reference assets. Report concrete failures and limitations; fix in-scope issues when implementation is requested, then recheck the affected behavior. Do not call the page production-ready if placeholders, broken links, or unverified claims remain.
